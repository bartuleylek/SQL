Filtering and Three-Level Sorting

Table: Products
Column Name	Type	Constraints
product_id	int	PRIMARY KEY
product_name	varchar	NOT NULL
category	varchar	NOT NULL
price	decimal	NOT NULL
rating	decimal	NOT NULL
stock	int	NOT NULL  

product_id is the primary key for this table.
Each row represents one product.

Problem
Write the SQL query to return the five highest-rated products that:
- belong to either the Electronics or Computers category,
- have a stock quantity greater than 0,
- and cost no more than 2000.

Return the following columns:
- product_id
- product_name
- price
- rating
Sort the result:
- by rating from highest to lowest,
- if ratings are equal, by price from lowest to highest,
- and if both rating and price are equal, by product_name alphabetically from A to Z.

Example
Input: Products
product_id	product_name	category	price	rating	stock
1	Laptop Pro	Computers	1800	4.9	5
2	Phone X	Electronics	1200	4.8	10
3	Monitor Plus	Electronics	500	4.9	4
4	Gaming PC	Computers	2200	5.0	3
5	Tablet Air	Electronics	700	4.7	0
6	Keyboard Max	Computers	150	4.8	8
7	Camera Pro	Electronics	1200	4.8	6  

Output
product_id	product_name	price	rating
3	Monitor Plus	500	4.9
1	Laptop Pro	1800	4.9
6	Keyboard Max	150	4.8
7	Camera Pro	1200	4.8
2	Phone X	1200	4.8
